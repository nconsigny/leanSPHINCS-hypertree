A stateless hash-based signature built as a hypertree: four layers of Merkle trees whose leaves are one-time WOTS keys, standing on a forest of nine few-time FTS trees. Figure 1 is the structure to draw. The stage further down plays one verification, from the message digest up to the public key.

How to read it

Hypertree
Trees stacked in layers. Each leaf of an upper tree is a one-time key that signs the top of the tree hanging below it.
WOTS
Winternitz one-time signature: a set of hash chains. The signature reveals one node per chain, and the verifier hashes each one up to the chain's end.
FTS
Few-time signature: 9 trees of 128 children, each child holding 6 short chains. The digest picks one child per tree.
Auth path
The sibling nodes needed to climb from a leaf to the top of its tree.
Grinding
The signer retries a counter until a hash has the required form. Counters are not sent: the expander finds them again.
